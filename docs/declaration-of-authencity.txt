Clarification of the meaning and regulatory implications of the Declaration of
Authenticity
The declaration of authenticity binds its signatory to the content of the statements for all legal,
regulatory, and disciplinary purposes in light of the legal regulations in force at the
University of Porto (U.Porto) and its organisational unit, the School of Economics and
Management (FEP.UP).
U.Porto considers academic integrity to be one of the most noble precepts of university
life and regards academic fraud and other forms of academic misconduct as very serious
offences.
The growing dissemination and ease of reading data, texts and graphics, accessible thanks
to communication technologies and the possibility of generating new content in various
forms, such as text, images, etc., using Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools,
contribute to greater recourse to plagiarism and falsification, in clear violation of
intellectual property and ethical standards.
It should be noted that the author of a research paper is responsible for its entire content,
even if Generative AI tools are used in its production. If a generative AI tool is used and
results in the replication of existing literature without proper citation, this constitutes an
abusive and illegitimate use, corresponding to a situation of academic fraud, and is in
violation of the provisions of Article 20 of the General Regulations for the Assessment
of First and Second Cycle Students at FEP.UP shall apply.
Strict compliance with ethical principles relating to authorship, academic integrity,
citation of previous works, and other ethical conduct required in the pursuit of scientific
research is mandatory.
By issuing this declaration of authenticity, the undersigned acknowledges the
acceptance of the principles of academic integrity outlined in the Declaration of
Principles on Academic Integrity of the University of Porto (ORDER No. 08/09/2011),
available at https://sigarra.up.pt/up/pt/legislacao geral.ver legislacao? nr=4939, of
which the Charter of Rights and Duties of the Academic Community is an integral part,
which includes the duty not to resort to academic fraud, under penalty of appropriate
sanctions.
The Disciplinary Regulations for Students of the University of Porto (Regulation No.
442/2011), accessible at
https://sigarra.up.pt/up/pt/LEGISLACAO GERAL.ver legislacao? nr=l 3853, defines
the disciplinary sanctions to be applied to those who commit academic fraud.
All detected cases of academic fraud will be treated rigorously and subject to
disciplinary and, where appropriate, criminal proceedings, with a view to exemplary
punishment of the perpetrators.
